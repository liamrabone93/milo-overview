The challenge

A question as basic as what was labour as a percentage of sales last week? spanned two of them and belonged to none — so it got answered by hand, in a spreadsheet, every Monday morning.

What we built

Four things, each replacing a job somebody was doing by hand.

The weekly scorecard

Leadership gets the full performance pack by email every Monday — net sales, labour cost, venue-level breakdowns — assembled without anyone touching a spreadsheet.

ToastHarriQuickBooks

Revenue forecast

Weekly sales predicted by day, using historical trading patterns as the baseline and layering in revenue already committed through pre-booked events.

ToastDesign My Night

Drink order recommendations

A purpose-built app that reads last week's sales, converts them to ingredient requirements through the recipe data, then adjusts for what's in the diary — so ordering is a quantity, not a guess.

ToastMarketmanDesign My Night

Executive & category dashboards

Estate-wide KPIs in one place — net sales, labour %, GP %, sales per labour hour, waste — plus the detail underneath: cocktail and draught performance, and capex tracking.
