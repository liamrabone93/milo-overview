The group

A national network, and an online platform that has to keep pace with it.

Perlas Network runs one of Lithuania's largest financial services, lottery and payment collection networks — physical distribution, payment collection and retail at national scale. loto.lt is its online arm, and the eCommerce team behind it owns acquisition funnels, conversion, top-up behaviour, segmentation and retention. Every one of those is a behavioural question, and behavioural questions were exactly the slow ones.

Weeks → Instant Time to understand a customer behaviour pattern, with no analyst iteration in between

The team was spending its time retrieving data instead of acting on it.
