The Case

Where Milo earns its keep at Juice

A couple of scenarios came up on the call, and both are natural starting points. The first is finance reporting: with a small team, the monthly and weekly packs take real time to pull together. The second is sales: Luke's reps want a live view of where they are against target, what's closing, and how they compare with the rest of the team, without waiting on someone to pull it.

Milo connects to the data you already hold and answers both in plain English. The proof point we keep coming back to: at Coolr, 20 to 30 reports that used to take days now come together in hours, and a finance director cut a three-day report to 30 minutes. Same numbers, a fraction of the work.
