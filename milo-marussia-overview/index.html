01 · Who we are

Milo is an AI layer that sits on top of the systems you already use.

The short version

We are a UK-based product company. Milo is our product, built by Autonomous Minds. It connects to the systems a business already runs on, then lets anyone ask questions of that data in plain English and get an answer with the source attached.

We are not a consultancy and we are not a reporting project. There is no warehouse to build and no analyst to hire. Most of the connectors already exist.

Who uses it

A mix of large FMCG and smaller operators who never had a data team in the first place. Kellogg's and the National Lottery at one end. Breweries, drinks wholesalers and hospitality groups at the other.

The common thread is not size. It is that the numbers exist somewhere and take too long to get hold of.

02 · What we do

Connect the systems. Then answer the question.

Three things, in order. None of them require anyone to change how they work.

One

We connect your systems

Finance and sales ledger, CRM, stock, spreadsheets, supplier and customer reports. Read-only. Nothing moves and nothing gets replaced. The systems carry on exactly as they are.

Two

Typed in normal English, in a browser or in Teams, Slack or WhatsApp. No report to build, no query to write. Every answer shows which systems the numbers came from, so it can be checked.

Dashboards stay live. Reports send themselves on a schedule. Alerts fire when something moves, so a problem finds you rather than waiting to be noticed in a monthly review.

03 · How we can help

What you shipped is clear. What sold is not.

Marussia sells a dozen brands through wholesalers, retailers, ecommerce and the on-trade. Sell-in is one clean number in the ledger. Everything after that arrives in pieces. Four places where time goes.

Depletions

You know what you shipped, not what sold

Cases out to Enotria, Amathus and the rest are clean in the ledger. What happened next arrives monthly, in whatever format each wholesaler happens to use.

→ Every depletion file read into one view, by brand and account.
Retail

A portal per customer

Waitrose, Ocado, Master of Malt and Harvey Nichols each report their own way. Rate of sale, distribution and stock cover sit in four places and never on the same page.

→ One line per SKU across every retail customer.
Brands

Own brands and agency brands, measured apart

Torabhaig and Akashi-Tai are a different commercial question to Foursquare or High West. Both get rebuilt by hand whenever someone asks how a brand is really doing.

→ Live brand view, own and agency, on the same basis.
Field sales

Call reports that never meet the invoices

The team already knows which accounts have gone quiet. That sits in the CRM, nowhere near the ledger data that would prove it and put a number on it.

→ Account activity and account revenue in a single answer.
Why it matters

Kellogg's had a sales dip. It was Coco Pops.

A region in the Middle East was down and nobody could explain it. The theories were the ones you would expect. Pricing, competitors, the economy.

Coco Pops simply were not on enough shelves.

Not a strategy problem. A distribution problem, and a question nobody could answer quickly enough to catch it.

Every drinks business has the same question wearing a different hat. When a brand softens, is it rate of sale or is it distribution? The answer is already in the data. It just takes three weeks and four people to get it out, by which point the quarter has gone.

Getting started

How it actually works.

An account manager does this with your team. Most of it is configuration rather than a project, and the heavy lifting is ours.

01

Connect the sources

Finance and sales ledger, CRM, wholesaler depletion files, retail and ecommerce reporting. Read-only or sandbox first, never live production data for a trial.

02

Agree the definitions

A case is a case and a depletion is a depletion, across every wholesaler format. Done with your team rather than to them.

03

Set the permissions

Role-based from day one. Brand managers see their brands, the field team sees their accounts, finance data stays with finance.

04

Rebuild what is built by hand

The brand pack, the depletion summary, the planning numbers. Run in parallel against twelve months of history so everything ties out before anyone relies on it.

05

Then it runs itself

Weekly depletion summary to the sales team. Monthly brand pack to the board. An alert when an account goes quiet. Nobody rebuilds anything.
