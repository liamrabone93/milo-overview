The challenge

Not a data problem. A queueing problem.

BetGames has never been short of data — it is one of the most complex data estates we work with, and one of our longest-standing. Play data at that volume answers almost any commercial question you can think of. The difficulty was that every question had to be routed through a BI team first.

5–10 days → Minutes Time from a commercial question being asked to an answer arriving

The old loop

  • Step oneSubmit a reporting request to the BI team
  • Step twoWait five to ten days for the update
  • Step threeReview a static dashboard
  • Step fourSpot the follow-up question, and rejoin the queue

What it cost

  • MeetingsCommercial decisions taken on numbers that were already days old
  • BI teamSenior analysts consumed by small adjustments to existing reports
  • OptimisationEvery cycle stretched by the reporting delay in front of it
  • CompoundingThe follow-up question cost as much as the first one

The solution

A conversational layer over the estate that already existed.

Nothing was ripped out. Milo sits across the systems BetGames already runs and answers on top of them — reading the warehouse directly rather than waiting for a report to be rebuilt.

How the team reaches it

Wherever the question actually gets asked.

Commercial questions rarely arrive at a desk. They come up mid-conversation, in a partner call, on the way to a meeting — so Milo answers in the places that conversation is already happening.

Chat Slack

Ask in the channel where the deal is already being discussed, and keep the answer alongside the thread.

Mobile WhatsApp

Desk Web

Full dashboards and deeper exploration when the question needs more than a sentence back.
